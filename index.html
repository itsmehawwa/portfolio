About

I’m Maya — I work across painting, illustration, and design for small brands and independent publications. My practice centers on texture, restraint, and the space between figuration and abstraction.

Commissions and collaborations are welcome. The fastest way to reach me is through Instagram, where I share works in progress and studio notes.
